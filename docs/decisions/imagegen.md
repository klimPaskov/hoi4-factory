## Decisions

New entries go here in full, oldest first.

| ID | Date | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-78 | 2026-11-16 | The focus icons in the 0.4.0 gallery look too dull and too alike. What happens? | Focus icons go back to the look they had before the frame and vanilla-look changes, which the maintainer liked better: livelier and clearly different from one icon to the next. The frames, the muted vanilla treatment and the simplification are undone for focus icons. The previous gallery's focus icons are the reference. The rule from the Irish feedback stays: no flags or national emblems unless a focus is directly national. After the maintainer approves a side-by-side sheet (old, current, restored), the change ships at once as 0.4.1, as a fix to a wrong result. The gallery's focus families are regenerated and the kit is re-pinned. Maintainer decision |
| D-91 | 2026-11-21 | How freely may agents use hoi4-imagegen, and who signs in? | Freely. Sessions no longer hold themselves to one request at a time. Several requests may run at once, across sessions too, and the tool's own pacing across its processes decides how many go out (W5-8). The general gptimage tool still never runs alongside it for HOI4 work. When the sign-in has lapsed, an agent starts the sign-in and completes it itself if that needs nothing from the maintainer, for example when the browser is already signed in. An agent never types a password, a code or any other credential of his. If the sign-in asks for one, the agent stops and tells him. A sign-in error is still reported at once. Maintainer decision |
| D-141 | 2026-11-23 | The sign-in has held at six requests at once since 21 November. May the route go higher, and what should happen after a sign-out? | The route has no fixed upper limit. With no setting it sends up to 10 requests at once across the machine. A project may set any other number, but a number above 10 takes effect only after the user confirms it explicitly, for example with a confirmation field beside the number or a one-time confirmation in the tool's login or status flow. Without that confirmation the route uses 10, and image_status says why. The 20 second gap between requests of one slot stays, and so does the back-off after a rate limit (HTTP 429). After a sign-out the route no longer drops to one request at a time for hours. Every sign-out lowers the number in use by one, down to a floor of one: from 10 to 9 after the next sign-out, then 8, and so on. That way the route finds the account's real limit and sign-outs become rarer. Once the user signs in again, the route carries on at the lowered number. It keeps that learned limit in its shared state so every process uses it, and image_status shows the number in use and why. Raising it again is the user's choice, under the confirmation rule above. The maintainer has confirmed numbers above 10 for ZIN (ZIN answer 210): ZIN starts higher, at 16, through the confirmation field in its project config, and lets the step-down find the real limit. The revocation record stays, and a sign-out is still reported to the maintainer at once (D-91). This replaces the trial limits of W5-8. The maintainer asked for it through ZIN's lead, first for ten or more and then in its final form (ZIN owner answers 205, 206 and 209). So it ships at once as 0.4.7 and does not wait for the weekly feature release of D-40. ZIN's lead asks him before ZIN downloads it. Maintainer decision |
| D-143 | 2026-11-23 | The server revoked the sign-in at 17:34 UTC after about 700 sheets at six at once. Can the tool sign in again by itself after a sign-out? | As far as it can without acting for him. When the route records a revocation, the tool starts its own sign-in flow once: it opens the ChatGPT sign-in page in his default browser and keeps the local callback listening for up to an hour instead of ten minutes. image_status shows that it is waiting for his sign-in, and since when. The sessions that draw notify him (a push notification from the session), so the only step left is his own. If his browser is still signed in, that is usually one consent click, and if the page completes without any step, the tool simply carries on (D-91). When the sign-in lands, drawing continues at the set number (D-141). The drawing lanes stop on a revocation as now, and resume by themselves once image_status shows a valid sign-in. The tool never types, stores or asks for a password, a code or any other credential. No agent clicks the consent for him, and the tool never retries the sign-in in a loop: it opens the page once per revocation. The maintainer asked for it through ZIN's lead. It ships as 0.4.8 right after 0.4.7, so the lock fix is not held up, and like 0.4.7 it does not wait for the weekly release of D-40. Maintainer decision |
