## Decisions

New entries go here in full, oldest first.

| ID | Date | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-37 | 2026-11-08 | When are the open 3D lessons assessed? | After the bake-off. The lessons in the shared 3D lessons log that are not yet marked taken (53 of 114 on 2026-11-08) stay open until the bake-off shows which rig and motion route survives, including the Tripo test once Tripo Pro credits arrive. Then each one is built into the shared Blender stage or declined with a reason, as 3D-12 requires, before 3D production restarts. No session spends effort on them before that. Maintainer decision |
| D-55 | 2026-11-13 | Which 3D generator does the route use now, and how is it driven? | Tripo, for every new model. The maintainer has Tripo Pro and is in Tripo's ACE creator program, so the 3D video promotes Tripo. Meshy is dropped for new models, and models already made with Meshy stay usable. There is no Tripo API key, so models are made in the Tripo web app. A modder can do that by hand, or let Claude drive the app through computer use in a browser when they want the work autonomous. The kit's 3D route gets a Tripo app workflow that is efficient and easy for other modders to follow. 3D work restarts now, ahead of the D-36 date, with one ZIN model made from its reference pictures as the first test. The test finds which Tripo models, settings and reference pictures work best, and whether Tripo gives better and more reliable results than Meshy for units, rigging and animation. More models wait for that result. Maintainer decision |
| D-56 | 2026-11-13 | What follows the Tripo test, and when? | The order stays: the Tripo test first, then the rest of the models, then the trailer. It no longer waits for about 2026-11-22. As soon as the Tripo test gives good results, the remaining models are made with Tripo: the rest of the approved ZIN references and the remaining Chaos Redux models, including the restored units that 3D-15 says to redo. ZIN carries on with its own 3D work on the same route. When the models are done, a new 3D trailer presents them and promotes Tripo. An agent drafts it from real captures and the maintainer reviews it (D-49). About 200 Tripo credits always stay unspent as a reserve. The open lessons are still assessed after the test (D-37). Maintainer decision |
| D-57 | 2026-11-13 | Which kinds of 3D asset does the Tripo test cover? | Every kind the route supports, not only land units: humanoid units, large creatures, mounts and riders, flying and winged creatures such as dragons, vehicles, planes, ships, buildings and landmarks. Hard cases such as planes and dragons are tried on purpose, so the test shows where Tripo works and where it fails. The trailer then shows every asset type. Where a type has no approved reference picture yet, one is made under 3D-13 first. The 200-credit reserve of D-56 still holds. Maintainer decision |
| D-58 | 2026-11-13 | How many Tripo credits may the workflow test spend? | As many as it needs to determine and build the best workflow across every asset type. This replaces the earlier 300-credit test budget. Every paid step is still logged in the shared ledger with the balance, the cheapest route that answers a question is tried first, and the 200-credit reserve of D-56 always stays unspent. Maintainer decision |
| D-59 | 2026-11-13 | When do the remaining models get made? | Not yet. The best workflow comes first: thorough research of what Tripo offers and how others make game-ready assets with it, a written workflow design that covers every asset type, and a test on one subject taken end to end to HOI4 format with rig and animations. The design is reviewed and put to the maintainer, and models in bulk start only after the maintainer approves it. Nothing is rushed. Models that are already built and wired are not remade, and their old input pictures are not reused. The dragon is the exception: it is built with Tripo from a new, more majestic picture that suits 3D generation. This refines D-56, whose order still holds. Maintainer decision |
| D-60 | 2026-11-14 | How do the open points of the Tripo workflow design get settled? | The picture gate list stands as written. A fresh reviewer approves pictures redrawn for 3D, and the process runs autonomously unless the maintainer says otherwise. The test orc is wired into the ZIN mod and checked there by ZIN's Linux worker in the isolated WSL copy. For the video it is acceptable that Tripo makes the model and the kit's Blender step rigs and animates wings, mounts and vehicles. The unattended mode may need Claude in Chrome, with the by-hand card as the fallback. The second armed test subject is picked by the session and shown to the maintainer before any upload. The route is built to be as deterministic as possible, so that a smaller model can make and wire a unit end to end: Sonnet first, and Haiku as the final acceptance test once the workflow is settled. Wiring covers the whole unit, including sounds, particles, map counters and registration, not only the mesh and animations. No bulk model work starts until the workflow is proven (D-59), and the credit rule of D-58 holds. Maintainer decision |
